1. Extraction of digits

n = 7789
r = 7789 % 10 = 9
7789/10 = 778
r = 778 % 10 = 8
778/10 = 77
r = 77 % 10 = 7
77/10 = 7
r = 77 % 10 = 7
7/10 = 0

time complexity - O(log10(n))   //because the number of iteration depend on the number of time the number will be divided by 10
